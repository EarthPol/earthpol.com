# Bookmark

- View the contents of a chiseled bookshelf.
- Left click a Chiseled Bookshelf while holding a **spyglass** to see the contents inside the chiseled bookshelf.
- Displayed contents: **Title, Author, Generation status** (Original, Copy of Original, or Copy of Copy). The slot of the bookshelf the book is in is also displayed.
- Book & Quill placed in Chiseled Bookshelves will display "Unfinished Book"
- Slots are numbered starting with 1) in the top left
