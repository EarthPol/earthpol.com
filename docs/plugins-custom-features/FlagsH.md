# Banner Flags

Using the FlagsH plugin, you can place banners and have them turned sideways to display as flags.

## How to place a flag
- Hold a banner in your main hand.
- Crouch and place the banner on the side of a block/fence/iron bar as if it were a flag post.
- The banner turn sideways and then display as a flag.

## Resizing the flag (Make it larger)
- Using a banner of the same kind as the flag that was placed, right click the flag while holding that banner.
- This will consume the banner and expand the size of the flag
- If you need to start over, break the flag and you will get your banners back.
