## Town Ruins Configuration

### ⏳ Max Duration (Hours)

**Value:** {townySettings.town_ruining.town_ruins.max_duration_hours}

**Explanation:**
This is the maximum number of hours a town will stay in the *ruined* state before it is permanently deleted. During this time, the town can still be reclaimed by its former mayor.

---

### ⏱ Min Duration (Hours)

**Value:** {townySettings.town_ruining.town_ruins.min_duration_hours}

**Explanation:**
This is the minimum number of hours a town must remain in the *ruined* state before it becomes reclaimable. This creates a delay to prevent immediate reclaims.

---

## Towny Religions

With Pantheon, an EarthPol-exclusive plugin, players, towns, and nations can reflect the religious beliefs of their residents. Players can declare their faith using:

    ``/res set religion [religion]`` – Choose your religion with auto-suggestions based on the existing religions in the religion list.

    ``/res [resident name]`` – View your current religion.

    ``/t [town name]`` – Displays the town's “Religion breakdown” on the Status Screen.

    ``/n [nation name]`` – Displays the nation’s “Religion breakdown” on the Status Screen.

As more residents select a religion, town and nation statistics update automatically, giving a live view of the community's spiritual makeup.
