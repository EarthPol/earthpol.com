# Downloading EarthPol Maps

## Overview
EarthPol has always been centered around history, with nations rising and falling, alliances shifting, and each version leaving behind a unique legacy. Players now have the ability to revisit these past eras firsthand. We have made our older maps available for direct download, allowing players to explore them at their own pace and on their own terms. Whether one wishes to walk through the remnants of old cities, analyze past geopolitical landscapes, or simply reflect on previous versions, these maps provide an opportunity to do so.

Furthermore, there are no restrictions on how these maps may be used. Once downloaded, players are free to upload them to their own servers, examine past builds, or even host private games with friends. The history of EarthPol is not confined to the past but remains accessible to anyone who seeks to experience it. Whether a longtime veteran or a newer player curious about previous versions, these maps serve as a means to engage with the server’s legacy in a meaningful and interactive way.
