# ⭐️ Voting Sites

Voting helps EarthPol grow by bringing in more players and you get free gold for doing it! Be in-game when you vote, enter your Minecraft username on each voting site, and click vote. Gold will be added to your inventory automatically. You can vote once per site every day, and the timer resets around midnight UTC.

✅ Make sure you’re **online in-game** when you vote.  
✅ Always have **space in your inventory** to get your gold.
