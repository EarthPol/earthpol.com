## Enchanting

### Storing XP
Storing your XP has never been easier! With a simple *Glass Bottle* and any *Enchantment Table*, you can save XP bottles quickly and efficiently:

- **Right-Click** the Enchantment Table while holding a Glass Bottle to store XP one bottle at a time.
- **Left-Click** the Enchantment Table while holding a Glass Bottle to store XP for your entire stack at once.

### Additional Changes
Experience cost limits are gone, so you’ll never see the **"Too Expensive!"** warning again. This allows you to fully upgrade your gear without restriction.

Armor enchantments are improved:

- **Blast Protection** and **Protection** can now be applied to the same piece of armor, keeping you safe from explosions.
- **Horse armor** is upgraded to accept **Mending**, **Unbreaking**, **Respiration**, and all types of **Protection**, making your steeds ready for any adventure.
