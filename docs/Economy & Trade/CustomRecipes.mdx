## 🔨 Custom Enchanting Changes

- Experience cost limits are removed. No more **"Too Expensive!"** when enchanting!

### 🛡️ Armor
- Blast Protection and Protection can be used on the same pieces of armor. Keep the TNT Carts at bay!
- Horse armor now accepts the following enchantments: Mending, Unbreaking, Respiration, All Protection types

## 🎨 Map Art
- There is a **carpet dupe** crafting recipe for each type of carpet.
- 1 carpet --> 3 carpet
- Due to this recipe, carpets cannot be used as fuel in furnaces, blast furnaces, and smokers.
