## Developments

### Vote Rewards
- **Introducing Vote Rewards:** Our new vote rewards system allows for players to have more choice in how they are rewarded for voting for our server. Upon voting, players will receive a vote token which can be used to redeem a vote crate.
![Vote Rewards](/img/vote_rewards.avif)
- **Vote Rarity:** As part of the vote rewards system, players will get to choose rewards that range from Common to Legendary. 
- **Vote Parties:** In addition to our new vote reward system, players will also be treated with an extra 2-3 vote tokens during a vote party. For a vote party to start, the server must reach a minimum of 100 votes.

<div style={{ textAlign: 'center' }}>
***Don't forget to [Vote!](https://www.earthpol.com/docs/vote)***
</div>

### SiegeWar
- **Siege Movement Restriction:** Players in peaceful towns engaged in a siege (as attacker or defender) cannot enter non-peaceful towns until the siege ends or their town loses peaceful status.
- **Siege Scoreboards:** A new system will track personal and global stats for each siege, including kills, deaths, damage dealt, potions thrown, TNT Carts used, and banner captures, with in-game summaries and leaderboards posted after each session. In the future, we will also be introducing a global stats profile that tracks your performance across all sieges.
- **SiegeWar HUD Realtime-Updates:** SiegeWar HUD will now update automatically. In order to see the SiegeWar Hud system, use the ``/sw hud [siege]`` command.
- **Expanded Blacklist of Commands in SiegeZones:** ``/tpahere``, ``/tpa`` and ``/home`` and ``/sethome`` are now blocked in Siege Zones (including claims) to prevent teleport abuse on banners. Previously, Folia allowed these commands despite plugin restrictions, but the new addon listener will enforce this change.
- **Set Drop Protection:** Players will no longer drop sets inside their own town, and the 4-chunk radius for keep-inventory will soon only protect set drops outside town boundaries.
- **Peacefulness Disabled with Debt:** If your town goes below a balance of 0 gold in your town, your peaceful status will be removed.
- **Surface-to-Air Fireball Buff:** Damage increased from 5.5 hearts to 7 hearts.

<div style={{ textAlign: 'center' }}>
***New to SiegeWar? Check out our [SiegeWar Guide.](https://www.earthpol.com/docs/War%20&%20Diplomacy/SiegeWar)***
</div>

### Quality of Life
- **Nation Flags on BlueMap:** Nation flags can now be displayed on your capital town in BlueMap! Leaders can link their account, upload a ``256x128`` PNG on the **[EarthPol Flag Uploader](https://earthpol.com/flags)** and showcase their nation's flag for additional diplomacy and roleplay.
![Update](/img/bluemap_flags.avif)
- **Towny Menus:** The new ``/townymenu`` command allows you to manage your Town, Nation, Plots, and Residents through a simple interactive menu.
- **Guide Improvements:** We are further organizing our guides to be more accessible, with our recent improvements targeting the **[Guide Hub](https://www.earthpol.com/docs)** and **[Custom Recipe](https://www.earthpol.com/docs/Economy%20&%20Trade/CustomRecipes)** pages. These pages now also include search bars for easier fuctionality!
- **Improved Copper Rates:** Mining copper with Fortune now yields significantly more drops, making it easier to gather resources for your builds.
- **Short Login Exemption:** Players logged in under 10 minutes no longer count as “recently online” for Mayorship or **[Honor Point checks](https://www.earthpol.com/docs/War%20&%20Diplomacy/SiegeWar#honor-points-system)**, preventing brief logins from holding land.

### Anticheat improvements
- **Anticheat Update:** NPC checks and silent-damage mode have been disabled to improve gameplay.
:::note

Minecraft 1.21.8+ is strongly recommended for PvP.

:::
### API Update
Recently, we added the following endpoints to our EarthPol API:
- **/voting:** Provides detailed information about voting, vote parties and historical data.
- **/sieges:** Provides live details of active sieges, battle sessions and etc.

These new endpoints will help the community as they build their own tools, such as vote party trackers, siege update boards, or custom notifiers. You can learn more about these endpoints and how they operate by reading our **[API documentation](https://earthpol.com/docs/api)**.

### Bug Fixes
- **TradeWar:** Fixed an issue that prevented tariffs, embargoes, and sanctions from being issued.
- **TNE Gold Bug:** The issue causing town and nation bank gold to disappear randomly has been resolved.
- **Improved Ender Pearl Behavior:** Pearls now cancel less often, particularly on small blocks like bushes and flowers.
