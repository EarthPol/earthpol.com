## Hello EarthPol!

<!-- truncate -->

Below, we’ll break down everything coming to EarthPol in this update:

## New Features
### Custom Recipe Additions
In this update, we’ve added more player-suggested custom recipes to make building more convenient and consistent. Below are the newly added recipes:

- Raw Iron and Raw Copper Blocks can now be smelted into Iron and Copper Blocks in a furnace or blast furnace.
- Added crafting recipe for Red Mushroom Blocks.
- Added crafting recipe for Brown Mushroom Blocks.
- Deepslate can be converted to cobbled deepslate in a stonecutter.
- Shelves can now be crafted using wood in a stonecutter.
