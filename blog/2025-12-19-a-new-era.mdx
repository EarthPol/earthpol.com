## A Personal Note

This is not an easy post to write.

As many of you know, I have announced my departure from EarthPol more than once in the past. This time, however, it is real. I am stepping away as I prepare to welcome my first baby girl into the world. Life is about to change in the best possible way, and my focus needs to be where it belongs.

Before I go, I wanted to leave you all with a proper goodbye and reflect on what we have built together over the last five years.

## Five Years of Building Something Special

For the past five years, I have had the honor of being EarthPol’s owner and lead developer. Alongside MrTytanic, Bustun, jhjhjh098k, Kauntar, and an incredible staff team, we built what I truly believe is the best version of EarthPol to ever exist.

EarthPol has never been just another geopolitical server. Since 2020, it has been a place where we tried things nobody else was doing.

- The first 1:326 Earth map
- One of the first three servers to adopt SiegeWar
- The original TownyJSONAPI, which later evolved into EPMCAPI
- The invention of Towny Apartments, which later inspired Quarters

EarthPol has always done things differently. Once you play here, other servers simply do not feel the same. Either people come back, or they start asking other servers to copy what we built here. That culture of innovation is EarthPol’s DNA.

## Versions, Lessons, and Project 2025

EarthPol has had many versions, but none stood out quite like V2, until now.

Toward the end of V6, after MrTytanic had fully taken the reins as owner, we reached a turning point. V6 was slowing down. Costs were increasing. The server was still healthy, but we knew we needed something new.

That led to a twelve hour meeting that resulted in what became known internally as **Project 2025**.

Project 2025 was a developers-only initiative, kept completely under wraps. We began planning in November 2024, started development in December, accelerated heavily by March, and ultimately launched on June 7th, 2025.

The goal was simple but difficult: design a server that could last.

## How We Changed Development Forever

One of the biggest breakthroughs of this version was not a single feature, but our process.

We built our own system of Developmental Cycles using GitHub issues. Every feature, enhancement, and suggestion was tracked, discussed, and planned properly. Nothing was lost to endless Discord scrollback.

At the time of writing this:
- 129 issues have been closed
- 76 issues remain open

Many of these came from our own noodles, but we did include a lot player suggestion. This structure allowed us to stay organized, onboard new developers easily, and deploy updates quickly and safely. Automation became a core philosophy. If something could be automated, we automated it.

This alone put EarthPol leagues above most servers.

## The Systems That Power EarthPol

The greatness of this version goes far beyond pipelines and organization.

### EPMCAPI

EPMCAPI became the backbone of EarthPol. It exposed real-time data from Towny, QuickShop, Discord, chat, and Quarters. This unlocked integrations that simply would not have been possible otherwise.

### BetterDiscordSRV

DiscordSRV was never designed for modern multi-server setups or deep integrations. It was clunky, limited, and difficult to customize.

So we scrapped it entirely and built **BetterDiscordSRV** from the ground up.

This gave us:
- Secure Discord OAuth linking
- Optional 6 digit in-game verification codes
- Deep customization without hacks
- Features like the Nation Flags uploader, allowing 256x128 banners on the EarthPol map

### Command Processor

The Command Processor bridged the gap between Folia limitations and external systems. It allowed commands to be inserted into a database and executed automatically on target servers.

This powers systems like voting rewards, where players can select from random rewards and receive them in-game within seconds. A simple concept that enabled powerful integrations never before seen in the geopolitical genre.

These systems are why EarthPol is set up for long-term success. They are foundations that future developers can continue to build on.

## A Transparent Look at Finances

Historically, cost has been the reason most EarthPol versions ended. Anti-DDoS, hosting, backups, and cloud services often pushed expenses well past $400 per month. When player counts dropped, the math stopped making sense.

This version tackled that problem head-on.

EarthPol operates from the Pacific Northwest, one of the most unique regions in the world for infrastructure. Through NWAX, a non-profit internet exchange in Portland, we are able to peer locally at incredible speeds.

Our setup includes:
- 5 Gbps upload and 5 Gbps download business-class internet
- Sub-millisecond latency to services like OVH US in Hillsboro
- Home-hosted servers with UPS and generator support
- Power costs around $0.06 per kWh

This allows us to run roughly four powerful machines for about $120 per month. Add anti-DDoS at roughly $80 per month and a proxy VPS at $25 per month, combined EarthPol operates at approximately **$225 per month**.

That is a massive improvement over previous versions that reached $400 to $600 per month.

## Sustainability and Growth

Lower costs do not mean lower reliability. They mean flexibility.

Savings are reinvested into better hardware, advertising, and long-term growth. Many of you may not know that I sell Earth maps at https://earthmc.org, and every dollar earned goes directly back into EarthPol.

Since June 2025:
- Total revenue: $4,281.33
- Average: $21.19 per day
- Monthly costs covered in about 10.5 days

The remaining time each month is reinvested into the server.

EarthPol is financially stable, technically strong, and growing. This is the first time I can confidently say that EarthPol is built to last.
