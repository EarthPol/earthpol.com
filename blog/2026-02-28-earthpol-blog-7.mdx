## Hello EarthPol!

As always, EarthPol continues to grow, evolve, and innovate. February brought the release of the Wilds and Wonders Update, introducing exploration-focused systems like NationPassports, Player Reputations, and improvements to TownyPorts and the lobby experience. Our community remained active across platforms, with steady growth on Discord, YouTube, and TikTok, while in-game engagement continued with our new interactive features.

In this post, we’ll discuss major server updates, show new developments, share key performance metrics, and provide a look ahead at what the coming spring and summer have in store for EarthPol.

## Server Spotlight

Welcome to a new segment of our monthly blog posts: the Server Spotlight. Each month we will highlight a special part of EarthPol to showcase the creativity and effort of our team and community.

### EarthPol Lobby

![New Lobby](/img/New-Lobby.avif)

The EarthPol lobby has been completely redesigned and is being built by our talented builder Flnb. The new lobby offers multiple areas for players to explore and travel to via NPCs. Visitors can engage in combat in the KitPvP arena or test their skills on the parkour course and compete for the best times. The build itself is highly detailed with plenty of easter eggs to discover.

This project is ongoing, and we welcome feedback from the community. Future plans include adding more parkour courses, reopening the EarthPol Museum, and discussions are underway regarding the potential addition of a duels system within the lobby.

*Special thanks to Flnb for creating our lobby!*

## Development

### Wilds and Wonders Update

This month we saw the release of Update Cycle 3, also known as the Wilds and Wonders Update, which was primarily centered around exploration and player interaction on EarthPol. Released in mid-February, the update introduced several major features and bug fixes that make travel, roleplay, and discovery more engaging:

- **NationPassports**: Introduced a system to record player travel across nations, adding stamps with visit dates and reflecting sanctions or travel restrictions.
- **Player Reputations**: Implemented community-driven reputations, allowing players to earn titles such as Trusted, Esteemed, Shady, or Infamous based on Fame or Infamy.
- **Preference Management**: Added a centralized system for controlling personal settings across multiple plugins.
- **Channel Management**: Gave players control over which chat channels they see, how they receive notifications, and how mentions are handled.
- **Special Bounties**: Added high-value bounties rewarding player heads for kills in the wilderness.
- **TownyPorts 4.0**: Overhauled the port system for mounted travel, improved clarity, guidance to port locations, and reliable long-term port storage.
- **Custom Recipes**: Added a variety of community-requested crafting recipes for improved accessibility and consistency.
- **Additional Features**: Included a lobby parkour timer, daily unique player counter, nation and town creation sounds, death message improvements, automated CustomLore purchases, and a new Discord suggestions bot.
- **Bug Fixes**: Addressed chat formatting issues, command conflicts, TownyPorts errors, and server stability problems.

This update strengthened core systems, enhanced the overall gameplay experience, and encouraged exploration and engagement across the server.

For the full detailed changelog, please read our [Update Cycle 3 Blog Post](/blog/earthpol-update-cycle-3) or watch our [Wilds and Wonders Update Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aZPUmUwrJrM)

### Additional Changes

#### VoiceChat is LIVE!
- Simple Voice Chat is now officially available on EarthPol. Players can use proximity-based voice communication to interact more naturally in-game. To access this feature, the [Simple Voice Chat mod](https://modrinth.com/plugin/simple-voice-chat) must be installed on your client.

*Please note that the [Server Rules](/docs/support/ServerRules) have been updated to reflect the addition of voice chat. We encourage all players to review the updated rules to ensure they remain informed and maintain a safe and enjoyable environment for the community.*

#### ️ Economy & Trading
- Forked QuickShop to implement needed Folia compatibility changes.
- Shops now automatically delete when a resident leaves a town.
- Added `/plot clear` support to manually remove leftover shops.
- Wilderness shops require administrator removal (or can be cleared after claiming the plot).

#### Towny Ports Update
- Distance limit adjusted from **2,500 chunks → 1,000 chunk direct teleport range**.
- Long-distance travel now automatically routes through intermediate ports.
- Each intermediate port collects its configured fee.
- Teleport now cancels faster when moving.
- Players instantly notified if they lack enough gold.
- Ports can now be configured with **0 cost**.

#### Lobby Updates
- ParkourTimer officially launched in the lobby.
- `/parkour leaderboard` added to view best times.
- Players now have **infinite hunger** in the lobby.

#### Server Infrastructure
- Installed two new battery units:
  - Backup power increased from **3.5 minutes → 35 minutes**.
  - On-site generator now fully supported.
  - ~10 gallons of fuel stored (≈22 hours runtime).
- Infrastructure expanded to approximately 6 on-site servers (proxy, backups, database, etc.).
- Achieved milestone: **Longest-running version in EarthPol history**.
- MySQL database cleaned to prevent potential data loss.
- New dedicated Suggestion system where suggestions now connect directly to GitHub via bot integration.

#### Additional Changes
- Added `/res rep bottom` to complement `/res rep top`.
- Mentioning players in EPMC chat no longer resets channel color to white.

#### Bug Fixes
- Sanctions and Embargoes restored and functioning again.
- Fixed passport point exploit from `/n spawn` spam.
- Fixed duplication bug with the “Hide Players” offhand item in lobby.
- Minor bug fixes following Update Cycle #3 restart.

### What's Next?
Update Cycle 4 is planned for release in **mid-March**. This next update will focus especially on the player economy, including balancing, refinements, and other long-requested improvements to EarthPol. More detailed information about the features and changes will be shared as the release approaches, so stay tuned for updates!
